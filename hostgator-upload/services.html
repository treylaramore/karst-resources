Services

Three ways
to hire us.

The audit is the first one. The fix and the monthly lead come after it, if the list shows the dollars are real. Not instead of it.

01

Audit

A first look at what you buy, the freight terms against how it actually ships, and the stock that is early, late, or just sitting. You keep a 90-day list with a dollar on each item, even if you never hire us again.

  • What you buy, and from whom
  • Freight terms against how it actually ships
  • Stock that is early, late, or just sitting
  • Two visits to the plant or the warehouse

Most audits fall in a range of $15,000 to $22,000. Half when we start, half when you have the list. We confirm the number before we begin.

02

The fix

After the audit, if the dollars are real. We renegotiate the suppliers that matter, put a simple scorecard on them, and close the gap between the order and the payment. We do not touch every contract. We touch the ones the list said were worth it.

  • The biggest contracts, not all of them
  • A supplier report card your buyer can keep using
  • You can pay less up front, plus a capped share of first-year savings we can show on an invoice
  • A saving has to show up on an invoice

A fix usually runs $55,000 to $85,000 over 10 to 14 weeks. The range depends on how many contracts the list actually names.

03

Monthly lead

We act as your purchasing lead when you do not have one, or when the one you have is buried in orders. A weekly check-in, a look at the large buys, and a calendar for the contracts that are about to renew. Your team still places the orders. We train the buyer while we are in the work.

  • We work beside your buyer. We do not take over the order desk.
  • Your team still places the orders
  • We train the buyer while we are in the work
  • Offered after an audit, not instead of one

A monthly lead is usually $8,000 to $14,000, six months at a minimum, paid at the start of the month.

Scope

What is included, and what is not.

In the work

  • Materials and the contracts behind them
  • Freight terms
  • Inventory
  • Large equipment purchases
  • Supplier scorecards
  • Training the person who buys

Not this

  • Installing a software system. We can advise. We do not staff the install.
  • Recruiting a purchasing department
  • A person sitting in the order desk at an hourly rate
  • A share of savings we cannot show on an invoice
  • Healthcare payer or Medicaid accounts
