Scope

Same questions.
Whatever you sell.

The questions stay the same, whatever you make or resell. If materials, freight, and stock are a real share of your cost, the audit looks the same.

01 — Materials

What you buy, and from whom.

The price moved. The contract did not. One supplier is still the only quote because rebidding felt slower than placing the next order. We read the buys that matter and put a dollar on the ones that are stale.

02 — Freight

The rate on the invoice, not the rate in the file.

The terms say one thing. The shipment does another. We check the class, the discount, and the extra charges against how the goods actually move. That check is part of the same three weeks.

03 — Stock

Early, late, or just sitting.

Safety stock set for a shortage that ended. Terms still prepaid on goods the building does not need. We price what to stop, what to return, and what should not still be on the floor.

04 — Contracts

The renewals nobody has opened.

Large buys, deposits that do not match the lead time, and a scorecard your buyer can keep after we leave. We do not install software, sit in the order desk, or take a share of savings we cannot show on an invoice.
